Verity.

Your privacy

Verity is built around a local browsing history and explicit analysis requests.

What is sent to providers

Claims and extracted article text are sent to the configured search and analysis providers, including Tavily, PubMed, Wikidata and Gemini when enabled. Selecting an area in the extension sends only that crop to Gemini for reading and starts a check. Provider keys remain on the backend.

What stays on your device

The website keeps up to 50 reports in this browser. The extension keeps up to 20 text reports. Screenshots are not saved in extension history. Browser storage is not encrypted by Verity. You can export or delete your history in the app.

Optional sharing and source icons

If sharing is enabled, creating a read-only link explicitly stores that report on the server. Anyone with the link can read it. Source favicons may load directly from a publisher’s website, with no referring page sent.

Your choices

Avoid submitting sensitive personal information. Use History to export or delete reports, and remove the extension to delete its locally stored reports.
